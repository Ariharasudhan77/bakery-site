# Ordering and pickup

## Order ahead
You can order ahead on our website. Orders placed before 6 pm can be collected fresh the next morning. We currently offer pickup only and do not deliver.

## Custom cakes
We make custom cakes for birthdays and celebrations. Please give us three days' notice, then call or email us with your idea, the number of people and the date you need it.

## Freshness
Everything is baked on the same day and sold until it is gone. Popular items such as croissants often sell out by the afternoon, so order ahead if you want to be sure.
