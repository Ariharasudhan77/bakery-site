# About Pistachio & Rye

## Our story
Pistachio & Rye started in a home kitchen with one oven and a jar of sourdough starter. The starter is now eight years old. We use slow fermentation and real butter, and we add nothing you can't pronounce.

## Opening hours
We are open Tuesday to Sunday, from 6:30 am to 8:00 pm. We are closed on Mondays.
